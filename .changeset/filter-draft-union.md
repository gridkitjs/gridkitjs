Breaking: `FilterEditorDraft` is a union of two arms tagged by `kind`, `ValuesDraft` (`kind: "values"`, holding `checked` and `includeBlanks`) and `ConditionsDraft` (`kind: "conditions"`, holding `conditions` and `combinator`), over a shared `FilterEditorDraftBase` (`options`, `blanks`, `search`, `columnType` and the new `openedShape`). The `mode` field is gone: `draft.mode === "values"` is now `draft.kind === "values"`, and a draft no longer carries the half it isn't on, so read `draft.checked` or `draft.conditions` after narrowing on `kind`. An action for the other arm (a value action on a conditions draft, `setConditions` on a checklist) starts that arm from the filter the editor opened on, as reopening it would show, and drops what the other arm held: values checked before switching to conditions and back are no longer kept, nor conditions set before switching to the checklist and back. `selectAllState` and `filterChecklist` take a `ValuesDraft`; the new `toValuesDraft(draft)` and `toConditionsDraft(draft)` return a draft on that arm, so `selectAllState(draft)` over a draft that may be on its conditions becomes `selectAllState(toValuesDraft(draft))`.
